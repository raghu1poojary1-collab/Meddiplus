Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026 · Application: MediPulse (medipulse-live.embarko.app)

MediPulse ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how MediPulse collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our real-time hospital doctor roster, medicine stock, and vaccine availability portal.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google OAuth 2.0, we receive your basic profile information as authorized by you: your full name, verified email address, and profile picture URL (email, profile, and openid scopes).
  • Contact & Verification Data: If you sign in with an email address or mobile number, that identifier is used strictly to establish your session.
  • Location (Optional): If you click "Near me" to sort hospitals by distance, your browser geolocation coordinates are processed locally on your device to calculate Haversine distances and are never sold or shared with third-party advertisers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your active patient session.
  • To display your name and profile initial in the MediPulse navigation header.
  • To personalize hospital search results and assist with appointment or token reservation workflows.

3. Data Storage & Security

MediPulse operates primarily as a client-side static healthcare portal. Your authentication session is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and can be cleared at any time by clicking Sign out. All network communications occur over HTTPS (TLS encryption).

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

MediPulse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell your personal information, serve behavioral ads, or share Google user data with data brokers.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can sign out at any time from the MediPulse header, which immediately removes your session data from your browser. You may also revoke MediPulse's OAuth access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.

6. Contact Us
